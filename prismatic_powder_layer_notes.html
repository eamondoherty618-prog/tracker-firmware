Powder Layer & Cure Notes

System Film Build Flash / Cure Note Other Notes
Primer thin-medium full cure per product Good default under steel work, and especially useful under textures / cast finishes.
Solid base coat medium full cure per product Use as your normal single-stage baseline when you are not stacking colors.
Chrome base thin 15 min @ 375°F PMT or 10 min @ 400°F PMT Prismatic chrome pages say fully cure before applying the clear / candy top coat.
Texture / cast finish medium-thick full cure per product Prismatic texture pages recommend a primer base under all texture and cast finishes.
Illusion base medium-thick Flash 5 min after it flows to gloss at 375°F, then cool Prismatic illusion pages say clear is required for the proper color result.
Illusion clear top coat medium-thick after part cools, then fully cure top coat Keep base and topcoat thickness even or the color can go blotchy / inconsistent.
Candy / transparent over chrome thin-medium full cure per topcoat Go light. Too much candy film darkens fast and can kill the effect.
Clear top coat medium full cure per clear Prismatic notes that clears can change the look of the base, so spray a test panel first.
Quick Build Targets
  • Thin: 1.5-2.0 mil
  • Normal: 2.0-3.0 mil
  • Thicker / texture: 3.0-4.0 mil
Simple Bake Rules
  • Chrome bases: fully cure before top coat.
  • Illusion bases: flash after gloss, cool, then clear.
  • Textures: primer under them is recommended on Prismatic texture pages.
  • Any 2-coat system: test panel first, because clear / candy changes the look.
